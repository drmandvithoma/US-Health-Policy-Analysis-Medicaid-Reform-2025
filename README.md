# Medicaid Policy Analysis (2025)

## Overview
This project analyzes proposed Medicaid reforms and evaluates their potential impact on coverage, eligibility, provider payments, and healthcare access in the United States.

## Approach
- Policy document review (CBO, KFF, CMS)
- Comparative analysis (ACA vs proposed reforms)
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated vs evidence-based policy summaries

## Key Themes
- Eligibility restrictions and administrative burden
- Federal funding and FMAP adjustments
- Impact on rural hospitals and safety-net providers
- Coverage implications for low-income populations

## Skills Demonstrated
- Health policy analysis
- Literature synthesis
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated content
- Healthcare systems thinking
